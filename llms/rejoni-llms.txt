# Rejoni

> Privately held, clinical-stage women's health medical device company in Bedford, Massachusetts
> (founded June 2020 by Amar Sawhney, PhD and Pramand LLC) developing proprietary hydrogel-based
> biomaterial therapies for gynecological surgery. Its lead product, the Juveena Hydrogel System, is
> designed to prevent the formation and reformation of intrauterine adhesions (IUAs) after
> transcervical procedures such as dilation and curettage or myomectomy. A low-profile transcervical
> catheter delivers two liquid precursors that cross-link into a soft, temporary hydrogel implant
> which separates the uterine walls during healing and resorbs in roughly two to three weeks — about
> one menstrual cycle — so no removal procedure is needed. Juveena is investigational only and
> pending FDA approval; the FDA filed the premarket approval (PMA) application in January 2026.
